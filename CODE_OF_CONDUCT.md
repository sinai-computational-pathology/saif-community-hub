# Community code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive scientific community. Participate with curiosity, critique ideas rather than people, disclose uncertainty, and make room for disciplinary and lived-experience perspectives relevant to demographic research.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate sharing of sensitive or restricted information are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community and research participants.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their verified institutional contact channels.
