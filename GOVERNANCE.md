# Governance and moderation

The Hub is maintained by the Mount Sinai Computational Pathology community. Maintainers curate the public claim corpus, moderate Discussions, and protect the boundary between public literature hypotheses and restricted research artifacts.

## Scientific decisions

- Community suggestions remain proposals until a maintainer completes evidence review.
- Literature plausibility and claim-level SAIF support are tracked separately.
- A proposed mechanism should include an observable prediction and validation path when the available evidence supports them; incomplete hypotheses remain visibly incomplete.
- Sources must support the exact public statement, not merely the general topic.
- Residual prediction motivates follow-up; it does not identify a causal mechanism.
- Self-reported race and ancestry are treated as contextual proxy labels unless a narrower mechanism is directly supported.
- Uncertainty, conflicting evidence, and unresolved confounding remain visible.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that includes sensitive data, personal attacks, unsupported medical advice, credentials, copyrighted full text, or material outside the project scope. Mature Discussions may be converted into Issues or pull requests for tracked action.

## Releases

The root site is a living edition. Paper supplements are immutable versioned snapshots. Corrections to a frozen snapshot are documented in the living edition and released as a new version rather than silently replacing files.
